4. SIZE ON THE WIRE (one full record, measured)
-----------------------------------------------
  CBOR 2334 B -> Deflate 1690 B -> Base64 2256 B -> 9 addon messages of 255 B

  of that: primary professions only   ~1670 B Base64
           secondary professions only  ~740 B Base64

  This is the largest realistic record. A typical member (one primary at 300,
  Cooking, First Aid) is about half of it. Recipe or item IDs above 65535 (new
  Forever content) take 5 instead of 3 bytes in CBOR and would make it larger.

  When is it sent?
    - to the guild once, when the owner's data changes (primary: within 5 s,
      secondary and rank-ups: batched per 60 s)
    - by whisper to one sync partner who lacks it or has an older version
  Comparing versions costs nothing extra: a sync first exchanges 16 bucket
  hashes (~150 B); if they match, no record is sent at all.
